# Recorder case V6.5

Two-piece TPU enclosure for the camera-free XIAO ESP32S3 Sense recorder, with a flush lid, internal locating lip, rear wire exit, microSD access, and a flat underside for mounting tape.

- Body USB/card opening widened to **12.1 mm**.
- Lid SD opening widened to **12.9 mm**, retaining **8 mm** inward depth.
- Both openings add **0.3 mm clearance per side** compared with V6.4.
- Fitted case pocket, **26.21 × 22.68 mm** outside footprint, and flat mounting bottom retained.
- Includes editable OpenSCAD source, both printable STLs, previews, dimensions, print/assembly instructions, and contribution notes.

The builder approved the previous printed fit apart from the openings and plans to trim that print. The final widened V6.5 openings are mesh-checked but have not been reprinted. This release covers the case only; it does not change or validate the recorder firmware, USB mass-storage access, wind-noise protection, or flight retention.

I'm new to 3D printing, so there's probably a better way to design this case. If you come up with a different or improved design, please submit a pull request! Issues with ideas, photos, or printable files are welcome too.

Print both matching parts at 100% scale: bottom flat base down, lid flat outer roof down with its locating lip up. Dry-fit electronics and confirm card/cable access before bonding.
