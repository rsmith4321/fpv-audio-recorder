# Contributing case designs

Different designs are welcome, including alternatives to the V7 sleeve. Submit a pull request to the parent repository, or open an issue with your proposal.

Please include:

- A short explanation of the problem your design solves.
- Editable source and printable STL or 3MF files, with units and version clearly identified.
- Printer, nozzle, filament, orientation, and the settings you actually used.
- Photos of the fitted recorder and any USB, button-access or retention checks.
- A clear distinction between CAD checks, physical fit tests, and flight tests.
- Credits and license information for any third-party work you include.

Preserve room for the camera-free XIAO ESP32S3 Sense board stack and its soldered wires. Keep the USB port, the B and R buttons and the microphone accessible, and describe how the case attaches to the quad. Do not describe an untested design as flight-proven.
