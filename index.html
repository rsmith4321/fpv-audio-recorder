The build

Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32S3 Sense, Sense microphone and microSD board, no separate battery. The camera is optional and is being removed to save weight. The flight controller will provide regulated power after its exact pads and available current are checked.

We intend to read Betaflight arm status over a spare UART. The recorder will not issue flight commands. Easy Eject will provide first-run firmware setup, temperature monitoring, and a safe WAV import path.

Progress

  1. Complete: microphone, microSD, USB transfer, and two-minute still-air bench recording.
  2. In progress: streaming flight firmware and Easy Eject recorder mode.
  3. Next: versioned one-click flash package and recovery check.
  4. Next: exact flight-controller power and UART wiring, followed by props-off testing.
  5. Next: mounted flight audio and thermal check.

Operating note

Give the powered quad airflow whenever it sits still, including while connected to a computer. The two-minute temperature result is a bench observation, not a flight-temperature or long-idle rating.

The firmware and Easy Eject setup are not yet released. No installer download is offered until the flight behavior and flash path are verified.
